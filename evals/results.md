**Script**

A stop-loss order is simply a price you choose where your trade closes automatically if the market moves against you. You set it before you enter, so you're not making decisions in the heat of the moment. There are two main types: a market stop-loss, which fills at the next available price, and a limit stop-loss, which only fills at your price or better. The limit version gives more control but risks not filling at all in fast markets. A common beginner mistake? Placing the stop too close to your entry, getting stopped out by normal noise. Give your trade room to breathe. The goal isn't to avoid losses entirely — that's impossible. It's to keep any single loss small enough that you can keep trading tomorrow. Try setting stop-losses on a demo account first. See how they behave in real conditions without risking real capital.
